Hydraulic conductivity data extracted from previous pumping tests and Geological Survey of Alabama (GSA) reports:
https://www.gsa.state.al.us/gsa/groundwater/publications

Example Citation:
Geological Survey of Alabama, 2018. Assessment of Groundwater Resources in Alabama, 2010–16. Geological Survey of Alabama Bulletin, 186, p.426.

Interpolation methods were used to estimate hydraulic conductivity values for locations where direct measurements were not available.
